# EverWear (Ideanation_Kucing_Cimit) — reference deconstruction

37 pages. Cover + 21 printed body pages + Daftar Pustaka (2) + Lampiran (13) + 1 blank. Printed page = PDF page − 1. A4, 869×1228px @105dpi.

**It is a report, not a brochure.** 14 of 21 body pages are 100% text. Photographs of products/people appear only on the cover and in the appendix. No full-bleed figure ever alternates with a text page.

## Answers

**1. Chapter opening.** Every BAB starts a new page (p2, p4, p6, p19, p21). Between the header band and "BAB 1" there is ~0.55–0.6in of paper; the title is two centred bold lines, `BAB n` then the chapter name in caps, and the first section heading follows immediately below with no divider, no big numeral, no chapter "poster". They pay for it: p5 leaves ~4.5in empty, p21 ~75% empty. Note the tension with Fauzi's comment 7 — the reference *does* start chapters on new pages; what it avoids is a heading stranded at a page foot… and it fails even that: p13 opens `3.3 Rencana Operasi` at the very bottom of p12 with 2.8in of white under it.

**2. Background argument.** Chain: global quantity → citation → demand-side attitude → citation → national channel statistics → citation → local condition (Depok kos) → citation → friction (`1.2 Komplikasi`) → one-sentence pivot "Everwear diharapkan dapat memberi solusi inovatif." Printed p1 carries six parenthetical citations — (Prajapati, 2023), (Global Fashion Agenda, 2023), (KLHK, 2021), (YouGov, 2023), (Sustainable Fashion Forum, 2023), (National Geographic, 2023); p2 carries two. Then citations stop: BAB 2–3 cite nothing, only framework names and competitor facts ("lebih dari 330k [user] dan pendapatan lebih dari 3 juta", p12). No "who cares" heading, no problem box, no gap table. The business is named once, at the end, and not again until BAB 2.

**3. Product explanation.** Prose. BAB 2 (p4–p5) has zero figures: Deskripsi Bisnis, Visi dan Misi, Deskripsi Layanan. The product is *shown* only in the appendix: **16 phone screenshots** (p24 ×4, p25 ×6, p31 ×6), plus 4 social-post screenshots (p28) and 5 campaign mockups (p27). No annotated screenshot with callout pins, no architecture diagram, no labelled parts list. Annotation is done by a numbered dot under each screenshot (p23).

**4. What their flow/timeline figures actually are.** Three distinct objects, none of them ours:
- *Service flow* (p24, p25, p31 — the same template three times): a grey serif wordmark, two flat colour lane bars (`Menjual Pakaian` / `Membeli Pakaian`), then **one vertical spine per lane carrying three dot nodes and a single thin arrowhead at the bottom**. At each node: bold 3-word heading + 30–45 words of real prose + one framed phone screenshot ~1.5×3in. No boxes, no diamonds, no connectors between nodes. Sequence is carried by the spine and by the screenshot.
- *Operating system* (Gambar 9, p12): a bordered container with a rotated left lane label `Proses Bisnis`, four white 1pt-bordered boxes, a green header bar `AI Profiling`, three bottom phase bands (`Akuisisi`, `Listing Produk`, `Proses Transaksi`), two merge diamonds, hairline grey arrows. ~6.3×2.5in, 7–8pt type.
- *Timeline* (Lampiran 9/10, p29): literal pasted **Excel Gantt tables** — months across, tasks down, pale green autofill. No roadmap arrow, no milestone pills.

**5. Appendix.** 15 numbered items on 13 pages, titled `Lampiran n: <Indonesian title>`, two to three items sometimes sharing a page (p25, p29). Content: product walkthrough, flow, TOWS, PESTLE, Porter, business-model references, campaign mockups, KOL table, 2 Gantts, financial statements, charts, risk. Figure:text ≈ 70–85% figure; pages 29/32/33 are ~100% figure with only the item title as text. No captions inside the appendix — the `Lampiran n:` heading does that job.

**6. Type and grid.** Single column, 1.6in side margins, 5.07in measure, A4. Times-like 11pt, justified, ~25pt line pitch (~1.5), 0.4in first-line indent, no space between paragraphs. Black body; colour appears only in figures and in `(LAMPIRAN n)` blue links. 1–4 bolded load-bearing clauses per page, bold also spent on product names (EverStyle, EverRent) and on competitor figures. Italics for English loanwords (*preloved*, *drop point*, *circular fashion*). Running header is **constant on every page** — sage band, white serif `Everwear : "Style with Care, for Earth We share"`, white pill logo right. Footer: cream wave band, `— Kucing Cimit` white italic, page number small at right above the band. Headings: chapter = `BAB 1` + `PENDAHULUAN` caps centred; H2 `3.1 Analisa Bisnis` bold, sentence case, left; H3 `3.1.1 TOWS Analysis` bold, one size down; H4 unnumbered bold run-in (`Kolaborasi dengan Micro-Influencer`). Indonesian headings, English kept only for framework proper nouns. Captions below: `Gambar 8. Competitor Profiling Matrix` bold centred 10pt. **No "Sumber:" line anywhere. Zero captioned tables in the entire document** — tables are captioned "Gambar". Numbering is unreliable: `3.1.3` twice (p6), `2.2.1`/`2.2.2` nested under `3.2` (p7–p8), `Gambar 1` printed after `Gambar 2` (p6), BAB 2 starting at 2.2.

**7. Financial section.** Numbers live in *prose*: funding split as a sentence — "Sebanyak 40% dari dana tersebut, dialokasikan untuk pengembangan produk, 20% untuk pemasaran … dan 10% disisihkan sebagai cadangan kas (LAMPIRAN 12)" (p18). No pie, no waterfall, no allocation table. Valuation likewise prose: P/S 1.5 → "Rp2,83 miliar", P/E 14.5 → "Rp20,23 miliar", IRR 28%, NPV Rp780,7 juta, payback ~2 tahun (p19). The only designed element is a **row of three KPI chips** at the foot of p18 — small grey label over a big bold number: `IRR 28%`, `Capital Gain 710%`, `DPR 10%`. The workbook itself is dumped in the appendix as green-headed Excel tables (p32, p34) plus three untitled bar charts with inconsistent axes (`Rp30.000.000` on one, `20000000` on another, `4E+09` on the next, no data labels, p33).

## Five moves to copy

1. **Quarantine frameworks to the appendix.** Body budget: ≤9 figures in the whole body, all in the analysis chapter, all real artefacts. TOWS, PESTLE, Porter, VPC variants, competitor matrix → `Lampiran A: TOWS Analysis` etc., 1–2 per page, 70–85% figure. Our revision carries 28 image objects plus 9 `Tabel` in the body — roughly 3× the reference. Delete, don't restyle.
2. **Replace every TikZ flowchart with the p25/p31 lane-spine template.** Build: wordmark, two flat colour lane bars, one vertical spine per lane with 3 dot nodes and a single 1pt arrowhead, and at each node a bold 3-word heading + 30–45 words + a real screenshot. `swimlane.tex` currently puts all five nodes in `mutedbox` at `text width=2.37cm` — 2–3 words per box, arrows doing the explaining. That is the failure Fauzi is reacting to.
3. **Every body figure must be a photograph, screenshot or mockup of the actual thing.** Not a diagram of it. Gambar 3/4/5/7/8/9/10 (VPC, alur, arsitektur, aliran dana, alur pesanan, value chain, Gantt) should become: real screenshots of the prototype pages, the real furniture, the real WhatsApp/OLX artefacts. Where no artefact exists yet, write prose.
4. **Adopt the heading and page furniture system exactly.** Indonesian chapter names in caps after `BAB 1` (drop `BAB I.` and the full stop), sentence-case numbered sections, no `&` or parenthetical acronyms in headings, one constant branded header band and footer band on every page, page number at lower right, captions `Gambar n. <sentence>` bold centred 10pt below the figure, never `Sumber:`, and **no `Tabel` style at all** — the reference captions its tables as figures or leaves them in the appendix.
5. **Write the financial chapter as prose plus three KPI chips; ship the workbook to the appendix.** Allocation and returns as sentences with inline rupiah, then one row of three rose pills (tiny grey label / big bold number). Reserve charts for the appendix and give every pasted Excel object a title and consistent axis formats — p33's three untitled charts are the one thing *not* to copy.

Copy the restraint too: 3–4.5in of empty paper at chapter ends (p5, p12, p21, p31) reads as confidence, not as a gap to fill with another diagram.
