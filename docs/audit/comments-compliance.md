## The 8 worst failures, ranked

1. **~27 body paragraphs end on a dangling hyperlink fragment** — *"…bertanya. Hasil survei"* (p04), *"…lengkap. Kendali mutu listing"* (p07), *"…tangkapan layar Alur operasi"* (p08, two back-to-back), *"…bukti Model kapasitas"* (p11, two glued), *"…di lokasi Definisi konversi"* (p17). It reads as a document with sentences surgically removed, because that is what it is. This alone explains "the revision is bad".
2. **"Lemari Sora" survives in 6 figures plus one appendix sentence** (p06, p07, p15, p16 ×2, p23, p31). Captions now say "kabinet contoh" while the bitmap says "Lemari Sora" — more visible than before.
3. **No numbers in the financials.** Tabel 4 and 5 (p18) are wholly bracketed: *"[Pesanan tahun 1 dan tahun 3]"*, *"[Rp per tahun]"*, *"[NPV, IRR, payback]"*. Lampiran E "Perhitungan Keuangan" (p28): 0 figures, 0 calculations.
4. **Market size is spend, not revenue, and the figure is implausible** — Rp40.500/capita/year cannot produce sales of Rp2,45 M cabinets. Deck S91 asked for three rupiah figures; the revision supplies three sentences.
5. **KIRI Engine is still named** (p19, p23, p24) against an explicit "we won't say it".
6. **Gambar A.1 (BMC) is broken**: headers clip at the frame — *"Hubungan pelang[gan]"*, *"Segmen pelangg[an]"* — and text runs out of its boxes ("…dukungan saat"). The flagship appendix exhibit is unusable (p20).
7. **The writing never moved toward the references.** Ch1 is 427 words of formal declaratives and one bar chart. Thrifty's ch1 is two-column, blue question headings, two annotated charts, callouts ("Good news: …", "Dari sini, kami melihat sebuah peluang"). Comment 17 unaddressed.
8. **Comment 7 only half-fixed**: p03 is 72 % blank (three lines); six pages lose 12–22 % of their height to figures that would not fit (worst p24, p31, p16).

**Honourable mentions:** Gambar 12 is a table numbered as a Gambar (p13); the funnel is named three ways — §4.5 *Awareness/Consideration/Conversion*, Gambar 17 *AIDA*, Tabel G.2 *Awareness/Consideration/Checkout/Conversion* (p30); §4.3 promises SWOT and delivers none (p14); the TOC sends Executive Summary to page 2 when it is on 3.
