## Q4 — Headings

Numbering **"01."–"06."** lives only on the TOC; in-body chapter titles are unnumbered. Deliberately **bilingual**: Indonesian skeleton + English subheads ("Industry Overview", "Unique Selling Propositions", "Positioning & Competitor Analysis", "Go-To Market strategy", "Loyalty Program"). TOC = chapter titles **ALL-CAPS** bold; in-body banner = **Title Case** in a light-blue rounded block bleeding off the top-left; subheads = **sentence case, blue, underlined** ("Tren baju second-hand terus melonjak"). Sections run mid-page when short (p10 continues p9) and start fresh only when the exhibit is full-bleed (p12, p17, p24–29). **The break is driven by figure size, not by the heading.**

## Q6 — Prose

Two-column justified body; **"Anda" and "kita"**; question-led openers; conjunctions doing work (Namun, Tapi, Untuk itu, Saat Anda…); sentences 12–30 words; paragraphs 3–6 lines inside a column. Em dashes and question marks are rhythm, not decoration. Enumeration stays inline ("Pertama,", "Kedua,") instead of becoming a naked bullet list.

## The 5 copyable moves

1. **Ban vector mockups; put a real artefact on the page.** Every Thrifty figure is a screenshot, photograph, native chart or native table. Instruction: for each Gambar, swap the drawn illustration for a real capture — app UI in a device frame (p7, p20, p26, p27), a real social post (p18), a real competitor logo (p9, p11) — and delete the generated vector scene.
2. **Rebuild market size and personas from BCC, with numbers and humans.** Instruction: TAM/SAM/SOM = BCC 91 circles with **"Rp x TAM / Rp y SAM / Rp z SOM" inside the rings** plus revenue-based top-down maths in the bullets (p12: "Rp13,5 triliun per tahun"); personas = BCC 97/98 with **real photos in circular masks**, three pill chips, income box, "Pain Points:" list (p13). No icon cards.
3. **One blue, one banner, zero invented decoration.** Instruction: one blue family + white + one grey; section openers = light-blue rounded banner bleeding off the top-left; subheads = blue, underlined, sentence case; key phrases get a pale-blue tint; delete sage/olive, corner swirls, link-styled cross-references, 3D clipart; hold 8–14% colour mass on exhibit pages, near 0 on prose pages.
4. **Write like p4, not like minutes of a meeting.** Instruction: open each section with a question ("Berapa banyak baju baru yang Anda beli setiap tahun?"), address Anda/kita, join clauses with Namun/Tapi/Untuk itu, keep sentences 12–30 words, cut BAB I citations to 2–3 per page, italic author-date after the full stop.
5. **Let one exhibit own the page and carry the numbers.** Instruction: giant numerals as the visual (p10 "50%", p11 "$168 juta"), stat cards showing only the figure (p6 "70%", "61,6%"), emoji quote cards instead of prose persuasion (p8), swimlanes/matrices from uniform PPT shapes — never a page of small sage boxes.

## What not to copy

Justified two-column rivers on p4; "Analisa" in the TOC; **"CONVENTION"** p17; "**7Bulan**" p22; "5 **Poter's** Forces" p24; pie label colliding with body p23; "On-Demand Warehousing" overlapping text p15; unfilled TOC placeholders p3; **EverWear's own copy in the p24 TOWS cells** ("Everwear dapat memanfaatkan…") — template ancestry, and a leak if reused; TAM ring reading "3,5 Triliun" while prose says "Rp13,5 triliun".

**Escalate, don't silently resolve:** comment 7 says "A CHAPTER DOESNT NEED TO START ON A NEW PAGE", yet Thrifty starts full-bleed exhibits on fresh pages (p12, p17, p24–29) and lets prose run on. Both hold if the rule is *"break for the figure, not for the chapter"* — Fauzi should confirm.
