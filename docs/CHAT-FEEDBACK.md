## Confirmed choices and implementation

1. **Near UI**, since it is the user's campus. Confirmed default is repeat local resellers for recurring supply, with outgoing renters featured.
2. **Align expansion with the funding roadmap**. Start in months 0–6, expand to close clusters at months 6–12, and run the physical activation from year two. Buyers can reserve for scheduled pickup and sellers can submit a listing at the activation.
3. **Cover**. Leave it blank for the user. Dense flow between chapters with the approved scaffold and no gratuitous gaps.
4. **Narrative**. Rewrite the opening and Executive Summary as a continuous business story, with product, fulfillment, marketing, and economics covered. Keep Sora and the scan benchmark out of the summary. Use the inverted triangle and real citations in Bab I, without introducing the company or solution there. Let the product become immediately recognizable in Bab II.
5. **Figures and chapters**. Open §2.1 with one explanatory paragraph and real screenshots. Put two-sided VPC in §2.2. Preserve the existing product journey and improve it with actual screens. Build technical figures for §2.4. Show who pays whom in §2.5. Redraw §3.1 handoffs, visualize §3.2 supply and resources, and show a heatmap in §3.6. Use PPTX templates for revenue TAM/SAM/SOM, STP and personas. Fit marketing execution to three consecutive pages. Keep technical evidence inspectable while making appendices figure-led.
6. **Technical direction**. User chose “build on LihatDulu’s existing local reconstruction experiments.” The finished own-stack direction is front and center, with third-party GLB test provenance accurately documented in the technical appendix.
7. **New inputs**. User supplied the interim CSV, confirmed the buyer service fee plus seller commission, and said the seller interview and workbook were both still on the way. They then authorized revising the DOCX and reading broadly, with subagents if useful.
